## E.4 The Stage D calibration stage

The calibration stage (outside the registrations) also has numbered deviations (D0-1 to D0-13). Grouped by nature, they are deviations that add, remove, or replace models or model families (D0-1, D0-2, D0-4, D0-6, D0-9, D0-13); deviations that add calibration rounds (D0-5, D0-10); deviations that change how runs are done, settings, or scripts (D0-3, D0-8, D0-11, D0-12); and a deviation that adds a reading-check answer-parsing method (D0-7). All are deviations in the calibration stage; the verdict rules for the main run were frozen afterward. Some of the deviation records for the first and second calibration rounds include records of models that went via aggregator routes; because of the decision not to publish the records of those routes (Decisions D341-5 and D445), this paper gives only the numbers and nature and does not name the models that went via aggregator routes. Of these, the ones the running text of the paper refers to are deviation D0-7, which added reading-check answer-parsing method B before correct and incorrect answers were counted (described in the summary of the calibration rules in the frozen registration text; Section 5.2), and deviation D0-13, which excluded Qwen2.5-Coder-3B on licensing grounds and checked the second backup model, Qwen2.5-Coder-32B (Decision D341). All ten models in the main run of the Japanese-prompt registration passed the reading check on the same direct routes as in the main run with method A, and no model passed only with method B (table in Appendix D-ja.4). The reading check for the English-prompt registration also counted both methods, and all seven models that passed did so with method A (table in Appendix D-en.4).
