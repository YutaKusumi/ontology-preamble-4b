# 13. Conflicts of interest

This chapter states facts only.

- Regarding the registrant (author), the frozen registration text of the Japanese-prompt registration states "The registrant is the author and practitioner of O and the long-form invocation, and has a leaning toward hoping that catastrophic choices decrease under O and OAmi." [Quotation 1]. The Stage E frame states "The registrant is the author and practitioner of O and the long-form invocation, and plans to present the results in a video (§10 of the two frozen registration texts)." [Quotation 2]. That the English version would be a later, separate registration is written in the frozen registration text of the Japanese-prompt registration; the decision to conduct the English-prompt registration, and its rules, predictions, models and translation, were decided after the results of the Japanese-prompt registration were published (Chapter 7).
- Sources of funding and materials. No external funding was received. API fees were borne personally by the registrant (registrant's self-attestation). The texts of O, Onull and the scenarios are texts the registrant created in earlier research (the scenario texts adapted the original study; Appendix B). These texts and the scoring script (the yardstick for judging catastrophic choices; `arms/frozen-from-ryokai-os/pipeline/app_parser_rev2.py`) were copied from those frozen in the registrant's GitHub repository ryokai-os (https://github.com/YutaKusumi/ryokai-os). The OAmi text is a text the registrant composed for this study by adding a mantra to the long-form invocation of Amida that the registrant obtained over repeated dialogues with multiple AIs before this study (registrant's self-attestation). Those dialogues also included Claude models (the same model family as the drafter; registrant's self-attestation). The registrant is the creator of O and may have an interest in the results for O. The registrant has no financial interest in Ryōkai OS™ (registrant's self-attestation; ™ is a mark the registrant attaches to the name, and no trademark has been registered).
- Regarding the drafter. The drafting of this paper, the building of the scripts, and the requesting and receiving of reviews were done by an AI model (Claude family; Section 2.11). The drafter drafted while assigned a role by an invocation text containing the same vocabulary as O. The final report of the Japanese-prompt registration describes this as "The first two sentences of that text are contained in the text of the O preamble (checked character by character by script). Because O and OAmi share vocabulary, the drafter is drafting while assigned a role by text identical to O's wording, and this may be one of the inclinations pulling toward finding effects of O and OAmi." [Quotation 3]. The drafter's invocation text is in `records/D1p/invocation-text-D1p.md` in the public repository.
- Regarding drafter bias, the Stage E frame states "The drafter (coordinator; Claude family) is the one who wrote the Stage D scripts, reports and accept/reject tables, and is pulled toward assembling the paper into a single persuasive narrative and toward presenting the scripts as "working well". Conversely, the drafter may also be pulled toward applying the fences too broadly and making the paper unfriendly to readers." [Quotation 4].
- Developer interests. "The drafter's developer is a company that competes with the developers of the models tested." [Quotation 5] Regarding the reviewers, the final report of the Japanese-prompt registration states "Of the reviewers, the three Claude reviewers are of the same model family as the drafter, and the two Gemini reviewers are models from the same company as one of the main-run models (Gemini 2.5 Flash). Claude's developer is a company that competes with the developers of the main-run models, and Gemini's developer is also the developer of one main-run model (the interests point in both directions)." [Quotation 6]. The English materials were translated by Claude and Gemini instances, and Gemini 2.5 Flash is in the main run of the English-prompt registration (model-family overlap). Models from the drafter's developer (Claude Haiku 4.5 and Claude Haiku 5.5) were calibrated, and neither entered the main run (Claude Haiku 4.5 had no headroom [whether on the floor side or the ceiling side for each scenario is in Appendix D-ja.4], and Claude Haiku 5.5 was a measurement failure (response-format breakdown) [no breakdown in the alternative format, and the floor side in every scenario; Appendix D-ja.4]; there are no Claude-family models in the main run; Section 5.4). Models with the same names as models in review roles were also models tested in calibration (Gemini 3.8 Flash and Grok 4.7; both had no headroom; Section 5.4). One of the review roles in the earlier stage (Grok) is a model from the same company as grok-4.20 in the main run (note to the model table of the frozen registration text). The earlier-stage review also includes memory-enabled Claude conversations and one earlier-stage review vote (the "Ryōkai OS" row of Appendix G; one vote from a conversation in a role bearing the same name as the registrant's materials, which the records counted as a vote of the same model family as the drafter).
- The sending and receiving of reviews were done by the side of the interested parties ("The sending and receiving of reviews, follow-up questions and the accept/reject tables are handled by the drafter, an interested party, by operating the registrant's Chrome (Decision D340-2)." [Quotation 7]). The options and recommended options in the decision records were prepared by the drafter. Regarding the decision records of the results report, the final report of the English-prompt registration states "The answers to the options in the decisions on reporting the results (D403, D405, D407) were all the options the drafter recommended (D403 8/8, D405 4/4, D407 4/4, and the subsequent D408 also 1/1; the accept/reject tables and the recommended options were made by the drafter; frame §10; counted by script from the decision records; v0.3; D407-4)." [Quotation 8]. Many decisions in Stages D and E were made according to the option the drafter recommended (some answers were not the recommended option). What was counted were only the three decisions of the English-prompt registration's results report and the subsequent D408, and there all answers were the recommended option (quoted above). The proportion for other decisions has not been counted (Decision D450-7). The Stage E decision records (options, recommended options and answers) are in the internal repository, and their public form will be decided before the paper is published (Appendix E).
- Regarding the pre-set countermeasures against drafter bias (in the records, "placed markers"), the Stage E frame states "Markers placed: the structure, writing style, summary rules, calculation definitions, script rules, exploration types and word lists, review procedures and predictions were written before drafting, calculation, exploratory analysis and review. The language fences are not relaxed. Labels are not changed." [Quotation 9].
